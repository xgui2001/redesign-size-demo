## Design Context

### Users
Technical hackathon team prototyping existing-furniture capture for an interior redesign tool.

### Brand Personality
Clean, minimal, practical. User explicitly says the UI should not be the focus of the capture demo.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light architectural workspace, restrained blue actions, plain readable typography, thin boundaries. Functional measurement diagrams only.

### Design Principles
- Put capture, corrections, and evidence first.
- Distinguish measured, imported, illustrative, and listing-derived dimensions.
- Do not present inferred product identity or image geometry as verified measurements.
- Keep photo data within the browser session; export structured dimensions on request.
